Office of the School Principal

Magdum National High School serves the youth of Magdum and its neighboring barangays with a full secondary curriculum under the Department of Education, alongside a strong Parent-Teacher Association that keeps every family connected to campus life.

This site was built to move the paperwork online — registration, PTA contributions, and records requests — so families spend less time in line and more time preparing their child for the school year.
